# Demo walkthrough (about 5 minutes)

Use this path to show the client everything that matters. Start from a fresh state (Profile, then "Restablecer datos de la demo", or open the app in a private window).

1. **Welcome.** Tagline, three value points, logo. Try the language bar (ES, IT, EN, FR): everything, including the mock data, switches. Tap **Empezar** / **Start**.
2. **Sign up.** Show that only email OR mobile is asked, with no ID document. Enter an email, tap **Enviar código**. Point out the inline error if the field is left empty.
3. **Verify.** Enter any 6 digits (for example 123456). Then pick **Madrid** (or use "Usar mi ubicación"). Three screens after the welcome, as the brief requires.
3b. **Guided tour.** The first time the user lands on Discover a 9-step spotlight tour explains the app. Show it, then skip it. It can be replayed from Profile.
4. **Discover.** Search "romana", toggle chips ("Abierto ahora", "Vegana"), open **Filtros**, switch to **Mapa** and tap a pin. Change city with the selector at the top.
5. **Pizzería detail.** Open **Forno Lavapiés**. Tap the heart (favorite). Open **Fotos** and **Reseñas**.
6. **Review = points.** Tap **Escribir reseña**, pick stars, write 10+ characters, publish. Toast shows **+20 puntos**. Then **Hice check-in** for **+10**.
7. **Recommend.** Back on Discover, tap **Recomendar**. Send a pizzería from another city. It shows "En validación", then about 6 seconds later a toast confirms **+25 puntos**.
8. **Learn.** Aprender, then Podcast. Open an episode, tap **Reproducir** (or **Saltar al final**). Complete 5 episodes to hit the **50 point cap** and see the friendly message.
9. **Jobs.** Empleo, filter by "Pizzaiolo", open a vacancy, **Solicitar este empleo**. Show the job-alert switch.
10. **My TPP.** The core screen: balance, level progress, caps per activity, then **Premios**, then **Canjear** a discount and show the code. Check **Historial**.
11. **Level up.** Profile, then "Añadir 100 puntos de prueba" a couple of times to cross Plata (150) and Oro (400). Show the level-up celebration and the locked rewards that open up.
12. **Theme.** Profile, then Apariencia, then **Oscuro**. Everything switches, including the map.
13. **Events and community.** From Discover, the green "Eventos pizzeros" card. Tap **Me apunto**. Show the Comunidad tab.
14. **Guest mode.** Log out, choose **Explorar como invitado**, try to favorite or review. The sign-up prompt appears.

## Known simplifications (say these out loud in the demo)

- Pizzerías, reviews, jobs and events are fictitious example data.
- Pizzería pictures are real CC0 stock photos, not photos of the (fictitious) venues. The same photo set is reused for recipes, courses, events and rewards, and the map is schematic.
- Point values, level thresholds and rewards are provisional until the client confirms them.
- There is no real backend: verification codes, validation of referrals and media playback are simulated.
